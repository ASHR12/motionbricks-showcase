How Neural Generation Works

  • Zero vision model inside the net: MotionBricks is a pure pose-to-pose generative neural network—no camera pixels or physics solver inside inference.
  • Exact inputs: 4 past frames (30 Hz context capturing posture & momentum) + 1–4 future target keyframes placed in world (x, z).
  • Output (~24 ms): Grounded 30 Hz chunk (24–56 frames) + neural foot_contacts (L/R heel & toe), prefetched & smoothed to 60 fps via Catmull-Rom splines, 120 ms Hermite inertializer & Foot-Lock.

Arena Layout Guide

  • Foreground Nave: Spacious concrete runway for open runs—flanked by the Vault Plinth (left), Gallery Bench (right), and center Overhead Duck Gantry.
  • Wide-Lane Props: Left-lane Low Steel Hurdle Rail & right-lane Stepped Concrete Plyo Block for wide-angle hurdle approaches.
  • Rear Stress-Test Gauntlet: Center rubber lane packed at tight 2.3 m back-to-back spacing (Hurdle → Vault → Duck) to test continuous multi-obstacle OBB chaining.

Why ~2.0 m lookahead matters: MotionBricks needs ~0.5–0.7 s (~1.0–2.0 m of runway) to naturally lower the torso or plant a takeoff foot before reaching a beam or wall. Per-obstacle cooldowns ensure triggering one prop never blocks the next prop 2.3 m ahead.
